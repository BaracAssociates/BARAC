About Us

Dedicated to healthier ecosystems and smarter growth.

At BARAC Associates Ltd., we believe ecological sustainability and agricultural productivity go hand in hand. Since 2013, our Guelph-based firm has provided strategy, research, and consulting to tackle some of today's most pressing environmental issues.

Backed by a team of seasoned researchers, we partner with clients to navigate climate change, optimize soil nutrition, and develop biopesticides that protect crops without harming the environment.
