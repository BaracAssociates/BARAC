About Us

Rooted in science. Grown by collaboration.

Dedicated to healthier ecosystems and smarter growth.

At BARAC Associates Ltd., we believe ecological sustainability and agricultural productivity go hand in hand. Since our founding in 2013, our Guelph-based firm has provided strategy, research, and consulting to tackle some of today's most pressing environmental issues.

Backed by a team of seasoned researchers, we partner with clients to navigate climate change, optimize soil nutrition, and develop biopesticides that protect crops without harming the environment.

Our Vision

To accelerate the world's transition to resource and environmental sustainability — by providing the research, consultation, training, and laboratory services that bring the best, most innovative environmental goods to markets on time and at lower cost.

Our Mission

How we pursue our vision

🔬

Research & Innovation

Encouraging participation of skilled, experienced, and intuitive professionals, and establishing our own farms and institutions for production and research services.

🤝

Collaboration

Forming nexuses with farms, laboratories, and resource persons — and networking with international research and business organizations to accelerate shared progress.

🌍

Sustainability

Monitoring global and local policy on resources and environment, and ensuring a well-committed, qualitative supply chain with lower expenses.

🎓

Training & Outreach

Providing training to young individuals and groups for technology transfer, and engaging in seminars, symposiums, and professional membership programs.

🔎

Transparency

Maintaining transparency and thoroughness in products and services — attracting opportunities for production, consultation, research, and laboratory work.

✨

Quality First

Providing quality products and services that meet the highest standard for clients across all markets, on time and at competitive cost.
